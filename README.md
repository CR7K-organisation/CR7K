# CR7K

## Community-Driven Project

CR7K is a community-driven project built around transparency, participation and a long-term vision.

The goal is to build a strong and transparent community around the CR7K ecosystem while progressively developing useful initiatives and community-led activities.

## Our Vision

CR7K aims to create an ecosystem where the community can participate, contribute ideas and help shape the future direction of the project.

We believe that long-term sustainability should be built through:

- Transparency
- Community participation
- Clear communication
- Responsible development
- Long-term thinking

## Community

The CR7K community is at the center of the project.

Community members can contribute through:

- Ideas and proposals
- Community discussions
- Content and social media
- Development contributions
- Community initiatives

More details about community participation will be published as the project develops.

## Transparency

Transparency is one of the fundamental principles of CR7K.

Important project information will progressively be made publicly available, including relevant project documentation, official links and blockchain information.

## Governance

CR7K is exploring a community-based governance structure designed to provide greater transparency and accountability.

Any future governance mechanism will be documented publicly before implementation.
